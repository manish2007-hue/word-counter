Our Mission

About WordCountr

Online word counters should be quiet, lightning-fast utilities that respect your focus and your confidentiality. Instead, the most popular word counters on the web have become billboards plastered with video ads, intrusive tracking scripts, and clunky interfaces that haven't been redesigned in a decade. Even worse: many tools secretly transmit your pasted text to cloud servers where it could be logged or scraped for AI training.

Why We Built WordCountr

WordCountr was built to fix every flaw in existing counters:

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: We don't have servers reading your text. Keystrokes are processed locally in your browser RAM.
  • Clean, Ad-Free Workspace: Zero ads around the editor viewport. Zero layout shifts. Instant page load.
  • True Offline Capability: Fully functional PWA that works on airplanes, in subways, or during Wi-Fi blackouts.
  • In-Browser File Parsing: Drag and drop .docx, .pdf, or .txt documents directly into the counter without third-party converters.
  • Academic Mode: The first tool that automatically detects and excludes references and in-text citations for university essay compliance.

Built for Serious Writers Everywhere

Whether you are a novelist counting chapters, a student finishing a 500-word essay, or an SEO marketer optimizing keyword density, WordCountr is your precision instrument.
