Knowledge Base & Search FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about word counts, character limits, document parsing, reading metrics, and writing analytics.

General Word & Character Counting
What is an online word counter and how does it work? ▼

An online word counter is a digital text analytics utility that calculates the exact number of words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and reading time in a block of text. It works using natural language tokenization algorithms that parse text across whitespace boundaries and punctuation markers in real time as you type or paste.

What is the difference between word count and character count? ▼

Word count measures the number of distinct words separated by spaces or punctuation (for example, 'online word counter' is 3 words). Character count measures every individual letter, number, symbol, and space. The same phrase contains 19 characters with spaces, or 17 characters without spaces.

Does a word counter count spaces, numbers, and punctuation marks? ▼

Yes. Groupings of numbers (like '2025' or '100') count as individual words. Punctuation attached to words (like hyphens in 'state-of-the-art' or apostrophes in 'don\'t') is treated as part of the word, while standalone symbols are not counted as words. Character counts provide two separate values: total characters including spaces, and characters excluding spaces.

How many words is a page? (Words to Pages conversion) ▼

In standard academic and professional typography (12 pt Times New Roman or Arial font with 1-inch margins), a single-spaced page contains approximately 500 words, while a double-spaced page contains approximately 250 words. Therefore, 1,000 words equals roughly 2 single-spaced pages or 4 double-spaced pages.

Document & Processor Guides
How do I check the word count on Google Docs? ▼

To check word count in Google Docs, press Ctrl + Shift + C on Windows and Chromebooks, or Command + Shift + C on Mac. You can also click Tools > Word count in the top menu. To keep a live count visible in the bottom-left corner while typing, check the box for 'Display word count while typing'.

How do I check the word count in Microsoft Word? ▼

In Microsoft Word, the live word count is displayed continuously in the bottom-left corner of the status bar. Clicking this indicator opens the Word Count dialog showing words, characters, paragraphs, and lines. You can also view it by opening the Review tab and clicking Word Count, or pressing Ctrl + Shift + G.

Can I count words in a PDF or Word (.docx) document without copying and pasting? ▼

Yes! You can drag and drop any .pdf, .docx, .txt, or .md file directly into the WordCountr Pro editor viewport. The built-in WebAssembly parser extracts the clean text stream in browser memory without sending confidential documents to external conversion servers.

Writing & Publishing Metrics
How are estimated reading time and speaking time calculated? ▼

Reading time is estimated using the global benchmark silent reading speed of 225 words per minute (WPM) for adults. Speaking time is calculated at 130 words per minute, the standard delivery tempo for presentations, keynote speeches, podcasts, and video voiceovers.

What is keyword density and why is it important for SEO? ▼

Keyword density is the percentage of times a specific keyword or phrase appears relative to the total word count: (Keyword Count / Total Words) * 100. In search engine optimization (SEO), tracking keyword density ensures target keywords are naturally represented (typically 1% to 2.5%) without triggering search penalties for keyword stuffing.

What readability scores should I aim for? (Flesch-Kincaid & Grade Levels) ▼

Readability formulas evaluate how easily readers understand your copy based on sentence length and syllable count. For general websites, blogs, and public communications, aiming for Grade 7 to 9 (a Flesch Reading Ease score of 60 to 70) ensures broad accessibility, higher dwell time, and clear comprehension.

What are the character limits for Twitter (X), LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok? ▼

Standard platform character ceilings include: X / Twitter posts: 280 characters; LinkedIn posts: 3,000 characters (with an above-the-fold cutoff around 140 characters); Instagram captions: 2,200 characters (first 125 characters preview); Google SEO titles: 60 characters; and Google meta descriptions: 160 characters.
