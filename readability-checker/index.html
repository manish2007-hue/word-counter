Algorithm Matrix

Understanding the 4 Readability Formulas

Flesch Reading Ease

Calculates ease on a 0–100 scale based on sentence length and syllable count. Scores of 60–70 represent plain English accessible to general readers.

Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level

Translates reading ease into equivalent US school grade levels. An 8.0 indicates an eighth-grade comprehension benchmark.

Gunning Fog Index

Estimates the years of formal education required to understand text upon first reading, focusing heavily on complex multi-syllable words.

Coleman-Liau Index

Relies on characters per 100 words rather than syllables, providing high algorithmic accuracy across programmatic and technical documentation.
