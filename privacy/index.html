Privacy Policy & Guarantee

Last Updated: November 2026. At WordCountr, we believe writers should never have to surrender their privacy or trade their unpublished manuscripts just to count words.

The Architectural Rule: 100% Client-Side Execution

Every analytical feature in WordCountr — from real-time word counting and syllable estimation to DOCX parsing, PDF extraction, and keyword density calculations — runs exclusively inside your browser's JavaScript environment.

Zero words, zero characters, and zero file contents are ever sent over the network to any server.

1. No AI Scraping on Your Writing

Many online writing tools secretly cache user submissions to fine-tune generative language models. We do not do this. Because your text is never transmitted to a server, it is mathematically impossible for us or any third party to scrape or train AI on your work.

2. Local Storage Auto-Save Only

To prevent accidental loss when you refresh or close a tab, WordCountr saves your working draft to your browser's native localStorage API. This data resides strictly on your physical machine and can be cleared instantly at any time.

3. In-Browser DOCX and PDF Extraction

When you drag and drop a Microsoft Word or PDF document into WordCountr, the document is read into browser RAM using local JavaScript libraries (Mammoth.js and PDF.js). The file is parsed without triggering an HTTP upload request.

How to Verify Zero-Transmission in DevTools

  1. Open Developer Tools in your browser (Press F12).
  2. Switch to the Network tab.
  3. Type or paste any text into WordCountr, or drop in a file.
  4. Notice the network log: 0 network requests are made.
  5. You can even disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane mode — the tool will work seamlessly offline.
